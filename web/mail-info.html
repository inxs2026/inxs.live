Why Google access is requested

With your authorization, INXS Mail reads messages and attachments, sends messages you compose, and changes message labels for the actions you choose. Access is requested through Google’s sign-in and permission screens. INXS Mail does not ask for your Google password.

Mailbox access requires the INXS workspace login. Anyone who has that workspace access can use the connected mailboxes. This service is intended for accounts owned by, or explicitly authorized for, the private workspace.

You can disconnect an account in Mail and revoke Google access at any time. Read the privacy policy and terms of use before connecting an account.
