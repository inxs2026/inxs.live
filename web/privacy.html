INXS MAIL

Privacy policy

Effective October 26, 2026

This policy describes how INXS Mail accesses and handles Google account data when you connect Gmail to the private INXS.LIVE workspace.

Data accessed and its purpose

After you grant Google permission, INXS Mail accesses your Gmail address, message headers and content, attachment information and selected attachments, message labels, and unread counts. It uses this data to display, search, read, and organize your mail and to send messages and replies you explicitly compose.

The requested Gmail permission allows reading, composing, sending, and organizing messages. The current application provides read/unread, star/unstar, archive, move-to-Trash, and restore-to-Inbox controls; it does not provide permanent message deletion.

Storage and protection

Your Google password is entered on Google’s own sign-in page and is not collected by INXS Mail. The application stores your connected Gmail address, connection timestamps, and authorization tokens in an owner-restricted database on the operator’s Linux computer. Google application credentials are kept in a private configuration file outside the website source.

Temporary sign-in state is also stored to validate the connection process. Send request identifiers, account addresses, and returned Gmail message identifiers are stored to prevent duplicate sends. The application also stores account addresses and message identifiers for messages dismissed from the INXS Trash view. Empty Trash clears that view without deleting the Gmail messages. These local dismissal records are removed when the account is disconnected. The service does not maintain a separate archive of your message bodies or attachments. Message content is retrieved from Gmail when requested and processed in the server and your browser. Files you download are saved on your device.

Website sessions use secure, HttpOnly cookies. Mail pages and API responses require the workspace login and disable browser and CDN caching. Messages display as text without fetching remote images or running email scripts.

Who can access the data

Connected mailboxes are available to anyone signed in with the INXS workspace credentials. The workspace operator administers the service and its server-side connection data. Only connect accounts for which this shared workspace access is authorized.

Mail data passes through Google, the operator’s Linux computer, the Vercel website hosting service, and the authenticated Tailscale network connection used by INXS.LIVE. These services process data to provide the requested connection and website functionality. Hosting and network providers may retain operational request logs under their own policies. Sending an email shares its content and attachments with the recipients you select and the mail systems involved in delivery.

Use of Google user data

Google user data is used to provide and maintain the Mail features requested by authorized users. INXS Mail does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or training general-purpose artificial intelligence models.

INXS Mail’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices, retention, and deletion

You can stop this application’s ongoing access by disconnecting a mailbox in Mail. Disconnect removes that account’s saved connection tokens from the application’s active database and does not delete your Gmail messages or your Google account. You can separately revoke the Google grant in Google account connections.

Connection records are retained while an account remains connected. Anti-duplicate-send records are retained until administrative cleanup or deletion. To request removal of remaining account-related application records, contact the operator at lexiinxs@gmail.com. Retained copies in backups, if any, require separate administrative removal. Deleting an application connection does not remove messages retained by Google or email recipients.

Questions and changes

For access or privacy questions, contact lexiinxs@gmail.com. Updates to these practices will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
