INXS MAIL

Terms of use

Effective October 26, 2026

INXS Mail is an email interface for the private INXS.LIVE workspace. These terms describe the intended use of its Gmail connection.

Authorized accounts and access

Only connect Gmail accounts you own or have explicit permission to use in this workspace. The workspace login gives access to all connected mailboxes; keep those credentials limited to people authorized to use them.

Your email actions

You choose which Google account to connect and approve the access in Google’s authorization screen. Review the selected sender, recipients, message, and attachments before sending. Read/unread, star, and archive actions are applied to Gmail. Existing Gmail apps and the Gmail website can continue to access the same account.

Use the service in accordance with Google’s applicable account and API terms. Do not use it for unauthorized mailbox access, abusive messaging, or spam.

Service limits

The Mail interface depends on the Linux computer, its network connection, the website hosting service, and Google’s APIs. Connections can expire or be revoked and may need to be reauthorized. If a send result is uncertain, check Gmail’s Sent folder before sending a replacement.

Attachments are limited to 2 MB total per message through this interface. Drafts are view-only and unsent compose text is not automatically saved. Gmail remains available for features the dashboard does not provide.

Privacy and disconnecting

The privacy policy explains how account and email data are handled. Disconnect an account in Mail to remove its saved connection, and use Google account connections to revoke Google-side access.

Contact

For support or questions about use of the private service, contact lexiinxs@gmail.com.
